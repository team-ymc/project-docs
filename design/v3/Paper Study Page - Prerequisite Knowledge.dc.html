1Introduction

Sequence transduction such as language translation, parsing and semantic parsing are key problems with many important applications such as conversational agents, question answering and summarization. Models are required to map an input sequence of symbols (e.g., a sentence in English) to an output sequence of symbols (e.g., a sentence in French).

The dominant sequence transduction models are based on complex recurrent or convolutional neural networks.

Recurrent models, such as LSTMs and GRUs, have been shown to successfully model such sequential data, but their inherently sequential nature limits parallelization and creates greater memory limitations, especially for longer sequences.

Attention, an integral part of sequence modeling and transduction models, allows modeling of dependencies between the input and output entirely by attention, without the need for recurrence and convolutions. The model, which we call the Transformer, is significantly more parallelizable than recurrent models, since all positions in the input and output sequences can be computed in parallel, in contrast to recurrent models, which require sequential computation. We achieve better translation quality while being more parallelizable.
